# ECO Technology — Operational Track Record

## Service Documentation System

ECO Technology uses GoCanvas for digital inspection reporting across all grease trap
cleaning and maintenance operations. Service records have been maintained since at least 2022.

## Confirmed Operational Facts

**Grease trap types actively serviced:** A-TYPE (small restaurants), B-TYPE (medium
restaurants, most popular), with per-visit inspection pass/fail status documented.

**Inspection workflow per service visit:**
- Before-service photo captured
- After-service photo captured
- Inspector sign-off required
- GoCanvas PDF generated and delivered to client
- Service book serial number issued per job for traceability

**Service volume:** Multiple client sites serviced per day, with sequential service
book numbers confirming sustained operational throughput.

**Client sectors confirmed by service records:** Restaurants, cafeterias, food courts,
and sweets establishments across UAE.

**Geographic coverage confirmed:** Active service routes in Ajman and Dubai, consistent
with ECO stated UAE-wide coverage.

**Municipality compliance:** Service records structured for Ajman Municipality reporting
requirements, with inspection status documented per visit.

**Field operations:** Named field technicians assigned per service route, with timestamped
log entries confirming on-site execution.
