## Executive Overview

**ECO Technology Environmental Protection Services L.L.C.** is an Ajman-based environmental services company delivering wastewater management, sewage infrastructure maintenance, grease control, high-pressure cleaning, and compliance-oriented environmental services across the UAE. Established in **2016**, the company supports commercial, industrial, hospitality, mixed-use, and institutional assets that require dependable service execution, emergency readiness, structured reporting, and disciplined waste handling.

The company is positioned for procurement use as a practical wastewater and environmental services contractor with a strong recurring-maintenance orientation. Rather than presenting an abstract sustainability narrative alone, ECO Technology’s operating model is built around service continuity, documented execution, municipality-conscious disposal, and support for one-off interventions, scheduled maintenance, and annual maintenance contract structures.

This master profile has been structured for use in tenders, prequalification submissions, proposal appendices, and client-facing capability reviews aligned with wastewater AMC and sewage maintenance procurement requirements.

## Core Service Portfolio

### 1. Wastewater and Sewage Management Services

ECO Technology provides scheduled sewage tank cleaning, desludging, vacuum tanker extraction, high-pressure jetting, deep cleaning of connected wastewater elements, and safe transport and disposal through compliant handling pathways. This service line is designed for assets where wastewater reliability is operationally material, including malls, mixed-use developments, hospitality venues, industrial environments, labor accommodation, and institutional facilities.

### 2. Grease Trap and FOG Management

The company supports grease trap cleaning, maintenance scheduling, and FOG control for food-service-intensive environments such as restaurants, hotels, food courts, and catering operations. This reduces blockage risk, supports hygiene-sensitive operations, and improves downstream drainage control.

### 3. High-Pressure Jetting and Drainage Maintenance

ECO Technology delivers industrial-grade jetting and drainage maintenance services to restore hydraulic performance, remove blockages, and reduce service disruption in occupied or high-usage facilities.

### 4. Waste Collection and Circular Economy Support

The company also provides used cooking oil collection and movement through responsible recovery channels, helping clients convert a waste obligation into a more structured sustainability and compliance workflow.

### 5. Compliance and Reporting Support

Service execution is supported by reporting-oriented workflows, maintenance visibility, and operational close-out practices designed for procurement-conscious and audit-sensitive clients.
