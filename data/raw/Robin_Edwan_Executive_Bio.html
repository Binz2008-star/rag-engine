He is the Founder and Managing Director of Eco Technology Environment Protection Services L.L.C. in Ajman, a municipality-approved environmental services company specializing in grease management systems, wastewater protection solutions, and environmental compliance services for restaurants and commercial facilities across the United Arab Emirates.

Through this role, Robin has developed operational systems and service frameworks designed to strengthen regulatory compliance and improve environmental performance. His work involves collaboration with municipal authorities, facility operators, and private-sector stakeholders to ensure adherence to environmental standards, pollution-prevention practices, and sustainable operational procedures.

Robin's experience includes implementing environmental management practices aligned with principles of pollution prevention, waste control, environmental risk reduction, and sustainable facility operations.

Prior to founding Eco Technology, Robin served as a VIP Relationship Manager at Ajman Bank, where he managed high-value client portfolios exceeding AED 200 million and developed strong expertise in client advisory, financial coordination, and stakeholder relationship management.

Robin combines entrepreneurial leadership with practical environmental operations expertise, focusing on strengthening compliance performance, supporting sustainability initiatives, and delivering environmentally responsible operational solutions across the UAE.
