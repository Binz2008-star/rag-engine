## Services

**Grease Trap Cleaning and Maintenance:** Monthly or scheduled servicing for commercial
kitchens, restaurants, hotels, and food establishments across all 7 UAE Emirates.

**Grease Trap Supply and Installation:** Supply and professional installation of grease
traps in 4 sizes: D (entry), A (small), B (medium/popular), C (large commercial).

**Marine Vessel and Tank Cleaning:** Professional cleaning of marine vessels and tanks
in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, and all UAE maritime facilities.

**Used Cooking Oil (UCO) Collection and Recycling:** Regular collection of used cooking
oils from restaurants and hotels. Recycling certificates issued to clients.

**High-Pressure Washing:** Industrial and commercial high-pressure cleaning of drain
lines, grease traps, surfaces, and equipment.

**Biological Treatment (GES System):** Non-harmful bacteria treatment that eliminates
odours, reduces grease accumulation by 85%, and extends drainage system lifespan by 200%.
